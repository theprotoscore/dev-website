About Me

Designer who codes, developer who designs.

I bridge the gap between aesthetics and engineering — making things that look great and perform even better.

Hey, I'm Franklin. I've spent the last five years building web experiences that people actually enjoy using. My sweet spot is the intersection of design and development — where motion, typography, and clean code meet.

I started as a graphic designer, fell in love with CSS, and never looked back. Today I specialize in front-end engineering with JavaScript, modern CSS, and performance-first thinking. No frameworks required — just craft.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me sketching UI concepts, exploring generative art, or chasing good coffee around the city.
